Why this exists

Nobody has built the India-first OBG reference

The app stores carry a dozen single-purpose "OB wheel" calculators and one protocol app. None of them carry the material that Indian practice actually turns on. That gap is the whole thesis — the same one PediAid walked into.

DIPSIIndia

India screens for GDM with a single-step 75 g load given irrespective of the last meal. Every imported app has only IADPSG.

PCPNDT ActIndia

Form F, Section 4(3) indications, and the penalties. The commonest reason a sonologist is prosecuted is clerical, not clinical.

MTP Act 2021India

The 20/24-week limits, the seven Rule 3B categories, Medical Board composition. Legally critical and constantly confused.

Anemia Mukt BharatIndia

Ganzoni iron deficit and IV iron pathways. Anaemia is the dominant Indian obstetric problem.

Pritchard MgSO4India

The regimen that works without an infusion pump — which is where most Indian eclampsia is actually treated.

Current editions

WHO Labour Care Guide has replaced the partograph. FIGO endometrial staging changed in 2023. FDA pregnancy letters are gone. Half the competing apps are wrong on these.

Live now

What you can click through today

Everything below is built and working in the preview build. Open it on a phone — the layout is the one that ships.

Dating & gestational age

EDD by LMP with cycle correction, by scan, and by IVF transfer. Current GA, term category, and ACOG Committee Opinion 700 redating cross-check.

Ultrasound parameters

GA from CRL (Robinson–Fleming) and from Hadlock biometry with a four-parameter composite. AFI and deepest vertical pocket.

Ultrasound guide

Eight scans — viability, dating/NT, anomaly, growth, Doppler, cervical length, fetal echo, biophysical profile. When, what is measured, what you are looking for.

Estimated fetal weight

Hadlock 1–4, selected automatically from the biometry entered, with a growth centile from the Hadlock 1991 reference and SGA/LGA flags.

Bishop score

Induction favourability with the ripening threshold and what to do at each band.

GDM screeningIndia

DIPSI and IADPSG side by side, with the translation between them made explicit. Glycaemic targets once diagnosed.

Magnesium sulphateIndia

Pritchard, Zuspan and Sibai. Pre-dose safety checks that actually gate the answer, the toxicity ladder, and the antidote.

Postpartum haemorrhage

Shock index, proportion of blood volume lost, the E-MOTIVE 2023 bundle, and the uterotonic ladder with its contraindications.

Counselling

Clinician checklists carrying the words to actually say, beside the words that land badly. "The pregnancy is not viable" against "I am so sorry — the scan shows the baby has died."

Landmark trials

Thirteen trials with absolute effect beside relative effect, because a hazard ratio without a baseline risk is unusable at a bedside — and limitations as a required field, because a trial without them invites the overreach that followed the Term Breech Trial.

What changed

A change tracker answering one question: what shifted in OBG practice, and why does it matter? The 1 cm/hour rule, FDA pregnancy letters, clomiphene first-line, the 12-follicle PCOS threshold — all gone, and many references still carry them.

Never Again

Anonymised composite safety cases with the timeline, the warning signs that were present, and what should have happened. The error is almost never a knowledge gap.

Laboratory reference

Trimester-specific ranges beside the non-pregnant ones, because reading a pregnant woman's result against the wrong column is the error it exists to prevent. Alkaline phosphatase trebles and means nothing; fibrinogen of 2.5 g/L at term is dangerously low.

Maternal immunisationIndia

Every recommendation names its country, organisation and year — India uses Td, not Tdap, and a test fails the build if any entry omits its attribution.

Drug formulary

Fourteen core drugs with every field §45 asks for — indication-specific dosing, dilution, contraindications, interactions, monitoring, antidote. Pregnancy and lactation are narrative, never a SAFE/UNSAFE label, and a test fails the build if one appears.

GynaecologyIndia

PCOS to the 2023 criteria, fibroids by FIGO type, endometriosis without needing laparoscopy, adenomyosis by MUSA features, contraception including Centchroman — which exists in almost no other national programme — menopause and urogynaecology.

9 reference topics

CTG, fetal growth restriction, PPROM, preterm labour, induction, VBAC, caesarean, GDM and Rh-negative pregnancy. Each opens on the numbers you came to look up.

Anaemia & ironIndia

WHO and Anemia Mukt Bharat grading, the Ganzoni deficit, and which iron to give when. More than half of Indian pregnant women are anaemic, and anaemia is what turns a survivable haemorrhage into a fatal one.

AnthropometryIndia

BMI against WHO and Asia-Pacific thresholds side by side — an Indian woman is obese at 25, not 30. Plus BSA and the ideal, adjusted and lean body weights that drug dosing turns on.

VTE risk & insulin

RCOG Green-top 37a scoring with enoxaparin dosing by booking weight; insulin total daily dose by trimester, with the postpartum collapse in requirement spelled out.

12 emergency algorithms

PPH, abruption, uterine rupture, eclampsia, severe hypertension, maternal collapse, amniotic fluid embolism, sepsis, shoulder dystocia, cord prolapse, ectopic, ovarian torsion. Each opens on the actions you take before you think.

FIGO staging × 5

Cervical 2018, endometrial 2023, ovarian 2014, vulvar 2021, and GTN with the WHO prognostic score — each with a note on what changed.

MTP & PCPNDTIndia

Both statutes, summarised for clinical reference with a verify-the-current-text banner. Quoted, never paraphrased into advice.

Frontend first. Nothing here talks to a server yet — every calculation runs on the device. Auth, bookmarks, Never Again, Academics and CME arrive in Phase 6, on PediAid's existing backend rather than a second one.

Roadmap

Built to the content specification

The plan now follows the OBG-Aid Content Specification — 68 sections, 30 modules, 100+ tools, with its own Tier 1/2/3 build order by bedside frequency. Section references below point into it.

What the spec corrected. Measured against Tier 1, the first build got the ordering partly wrong: five FIGO staging systems shipped (Tier 3, §66) while none of the eleven Tier 1 emergency algorithms exist. Good content, wrong slot. The spec also requires two things nothing built so far had — a versioned content record on every item (§62) and an interconnection graph (§68). Both are now in, because retrofitting either across 100+ tools is the expensive path.

Governance

The spec's rules, enforced by the build

§61 says never invent a dose, a trial result, a reference range or a staging criterion, and make every claim traceable. §63 names eighteen categories that cannot be published without a clinician's sign-off. Those are rules in a document until something checks them — so twelve tests now do, and a failing check blocks the deploy.

Emergencies open on the action

A red "do this now" block, then §52's ten sections collapsed beneath. Explicit do-not steps get their own treatment — fundal pressure in dystocia, fluid loading in eclampsia — because the harmful action kills more reliably than the omitted one.

Every item is a versioned record

ID, author, medical reviewer, source organisation and title, publication year, version, evidence level, created and next-review dates, status. Rendered at the foot of every clinical screen.

Nothing is an island

Every item declares what it connects to and why — the reason belongs to the edge. Dating links to the MTP Act because the 20 and 24-week limits turn entirely on accurate dating. A test fails if any link dangles.

Unreviewed content says so

34 items are on the §63 list and none is signed off yet — every emergency algorithm, magnesium sulphate, PPH, both statutes, and everything carrying drug or anticoagulation dosing. Each carries a banner saying so where the doses are, and the drawer holds the queue. A silent gap is the dangerous kind.

Identity

Locked, and locked identically

PediAid shipped com.pediaid.pediaid on Android and app.pediaid.pediaid on iOS. That mismatch is permanent. Flutter's own scaffold reproduced exactly the same divergence here — it was caught and corrected before any upload exists.
